# Challenge to the T3.2.10 Architecture Review

**Reviewing** `t3.2.10-architecture-review.md` · **Date** 2026-08-06
**Posture** Adversarial. The review is treated as a proposal to be defeated, not a decision to be ratified.

**Headline: the review is directionally sound and tactically wrong.** It found
three real defects, then wrapped them in nine recommendations that a POC does not
need, and it contradicted a risk the same author had already logged as High.
**Four of its twelve required modifications should be rejected outright, three
postponed, and only five kept.**

---

## 1. Challenging the Review

### 1.1 What I agree with

| # | Item | Why it survives |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **`interestRate` can contradict `tenureRates`** | Real. Two screens quoting different rates for one deposit is a correctness defect, not a style question. *But the fix is wrong — see D2.* |
| **A2** | **Fractional rupees accepted into the draft** (M7) | Real, live, and one `.int()`. `tenureMonths` has `.int()`; `amount` does not. Indefensible on a financial platform |
| **A3** | **Duplicate rate rows resolve silently** (M9) | Real. `find` returns the first; a duplicate is a wrong rate with no error |
| **A4** | **Compounding frequency is a hardcoded platform constant** | The review's best catch. `COMPOUNDS_PER_YEAR = 4` answers a per-bank contract question globally. Correct for all six current products, wrong the moment a bank compounds monthly |
| **A5** | **Maturity Date and Effective Annual Yield are spec-required and absent** | Real gap against spec 21b §E/§6 |
| **A6** | **Screen-reader validation of the dual chip+slider control** (M12) | Two focusable controls reporting one value is unusual enough to verify rather than assert |
| **A7** | **`minInvestment` may vary by tenure** (M11) | A legitimate question with retroactive reach into approved T3.2.8 |

### 1.2 What I disagree with

**D1 — "M3: adopt `RateQuote` before T3.2.10." Reject.** Full argument in §2.
Summary: it invents a shape for four requirements nobody has specified, and the
migration-cost argument is arithmetically weak.

**D2 — "M4/M5/M6: delete the duplicated fields." Reject as stated; the diagnosis
is right and the prescription would break approved UI.**

This is the review's most serious error, and it is a *category* mistake — it
treated "two fields describe related things" as "duplication of one fact".

- **M5, delete `payoutOptions: string[]`.** The Overview rail renders *"Monthly,
  Quarterly, Cumulative, At Maturity"* — the bank's own four marketing words,
  including "Cumulative", which has no id because it is a synonym. Deriving from
  `supportedPayouts` yields *"At Maturity, Monthly, Quarterly"*. **That is a
  change to approved UI copy**, which the standing instruction forbids. The
  review recommended a redesign while citing the rule against redesign.
- **M6, delete `tenureRange`.** Overview shows *"7 Days – 10 Years"*. Deriving
  from a one-row card yields *"24 months"*. Same violation. Worse, the two may be
  **legitimately different facts**: the range a product family advertises versus
  the terms currently bookable. A bank can advertise 7 days–10 years and have
  only some tenures open today.
- **M4, derive `interestRate`.** The risk is real; the structural change is not
  the cheapest fix.

**Alternative (see §4): keep all three fields, and make ownership explicit
through an invariant rather than a deletion.** One probe — *"no behavioural code
path reads `payoutOptions`, `tenureRange` or `interestRate`"* — buys the same
protection at a fraction of the cost and changes no pixels.

**D3 — "M1: `tenureDays` before T3.2.10." Postpone, and the review's urgency
argument does not hold.**

The review claimed the cost rises with delay. It does not, materially:

- The persisted draft lives in **`sessionStorage` with a 24-hour expiry and a
  version-gated fail-closed parse**. Migrating it costs a version bump; old
  drafts are discarded, which is already the designed behaviour. **Migration cost
  ≈ zero, now or later.**
- `tenureMonths` funnels through three functions (`resolveTenureRate`,
  `selectableTenures`, `validateTenure`) plus one row builder. Adding a tenure
  control adds *one consumer*, not a class of them.

**D4 — the review conflated Maturity Date with the tenure unit. This is an
error.** It asserted that Maturity Date "is not computable" and that this is "the
same question" as days-vs-months. It is not: `valueDate + N months` is a
perfectly well-defined date operation. Maturity Date is blocked on the absence of
a **value date**, not on the unit. The two questions are independent, and
bundling them inflated the case for `tenureDays`.

**D5 — the review ignored a High risk its own author had logged.**
`t3.2-rebaseline.md` §4 R-F3 states: *"Effort is going into booking configuration
while the Note reprioritises toward differentiators… none of the seven must-have
features is booking configuration."* The review then demanded twelve further
modifications to booking configuration. See §8.

### 1.3 What I would postpone

| # | Item | Until |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | `RateQuote` / `resolveRate` | **R1**, or the first real second dimension (senior rates or payout pricing), whichever is first |
| **P2** | `tenureDays` migration | The first product that actually sells a sub-month tenure. Trigger, not schedule |
| **P3** | Day-count convention | Bundled with P2. Irrelevant while every tenure is a whole month and interest is computed as `months/12` |
| **P4** | `useInvestmentConfiguration` extraction | Genuinely useful, genuinely not urgent. Do it when the screen next needs a change, not as a prerequisite |
| **P5** | Applied-rate capture in the configuration | Before Review & Confirm, as the review said. Correctly placed |

### 1.4 What should be rejected

| # | Item | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **M3 `RateQuote` as a T3.2.10 blocker** | Speculative generality. §2 |
| **R2** | **M5 delete `payoutOptions`** | Would change approved UI copy |
| **R3** | **M6 delete `tenureRange`** | Same, and the fields may be genuinely distinct |
| **R4** | **M8 "one draft-schema version bump covering M1 and M7"** | Couples an urgent one-line fix (integer rupees) to a postponed migration. Ship M7 alone, today |

### 1.5 Alternative designs

**ALT-1 — Ownership invariant instead of field deletion.** Replace M4/M5/M6
with a single verification probe asserting that `interestRate`, `tenureRange` and
`payoutOptions` are read **only** by the two Overview row builders, never by
validation, pricing or selection. Cost: one probe. Protection: the same defect
becomes a build failure. Pixels changed: zero.

**ALT-2 — Consistency assertion instead of derivation.** Add a fixture-level (and
later ingestion-level) assertion: the headline `interestRate` must appear
somewhere in `tenureRates`. Catches the drift the review feared without making
one field a function of another.

**ALT-3 — `label?: string` on `TenureRate`, now.** The one piece of M1 worth
taking immediately. Zero migration, no schema change, and it lets a bank render
"1 Year" or "7 Days" in its own words the day a card arrives. It also removes the
main argument for a `{value, unit}` duration object.

**ALT-4 — `compoundingFrequency` on the product, not on a `RateQuote`.** The
review's genuine catch (A4) does not require its proposed structure. One optional
field on `DepositTerms`, defaulting to quarterly, fixes the per-bank problem
without inventing a resolution protocol.

## 2. Is `RateQuote` Actually Worth It?

**Verdict: not now. Adopt at R1.**

### 2.1 Assessment by principle

| Lens | Reading |
|---|---|
| **SOLID — OCP** | Cited *for* `RateQuote`: new dimensions become fields, not signature changes. **But OCP is about extension points you have evidence you need.** Four speculative dimensions is not evidence |
| **SOLID — ISP** | Argues *against*. Today every caller wants one number. A quote object forces all three call sites to accept five fields to use one |
| **DDD** | The strongest argument *for*. A quote genuinely is a value object with provenance, and "the rate we quoted, when, from which band" is real ubiquitous language. **But DDD also says model the domain you have.** Effective dating is not in the domain until rates change dynamically, which requires an API that does not exist |
| **Clean Architecture** | Neutral. `resolveTenureRate` is already a pure domain function behind a stable boundary. Its *return type* is an implementation detail of that boundary |
| **Evolutionary Architecture** | Argues *against, decisively*. The test of a good evolutionary decision is whether it is **reversible and last-responsible-moment**. Changing a return type consumed by three call sites that all do `if (rate === null) …` is about as reversible as a change gets. The last responsible moment is the first real second dimension |
| **YAGNI** | Argues against, plainly. Four requirements, zero specifications, zero customers |
| **KISS** | Argues against. `number \| null` is the simplest thing that works and is fully tested |

### 2.2 The migration-cost argument, checked

The review said *"three call sites today, nine after T3.2.10."*

Counted honestly, T3.2.10 adds **one** consumer of the resolved rate (the tenure
selector, and even that consumes `selectableTenures`, not the rate). The rail and
the engine already exist. The real progression is 3 → 4, not 3 → 9. **The
review's central quantitative claim is wrong by roughly a factor of two**, and it
was the claim carrying the "do it now" conclusion.

### 2.3 Costs and savings

| | Now | At R1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Migration cost** | ~4h: type, rename, 3 call sites, fixture, ~15 assertions rewritten | ~6h: same plus one more call site, done alongside the `rates.ts` consolidation already scheduled |
| **Marginal cost of waiting** | — | **≈ 2h** |
| **Future savings** | Identical in both cases | Identical |
| **Risk of doing it now** | Shape invented without requirements; likely wrong on first contact with a real rate API; a second migration | — |

**Two hours of marginal cost does not justify designing against four unspecified
requirements.** The saving the review claimed is real but is not lost by waiting.

### 2.4 What to do instead

- Keep `resolveTenureRate → number | null`
- Take **ALT-4**: `compoundingFrequency?` on `DepositTerms` — this closes the one
  *real* defect (A4) that motivated the quote object
- Write `RateQuote` into `shared-finance-layer.md` as the **R1 target shape**, so
  the design is agreed and the implementation is deferred

## 4. `DepositTerms` Field Audit

Every field, classified.

| Field | Classification | Dangerous? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `interestRate: number` | **Display copy** (headline / advertised) | ⚠ **Yes** — can contradict `tenureRates`; read by the Overview rail *and* by the configured rail's no-tenure-selected branch | **Keep**, add ALT-2 consistency assertion + ALT-1 read-restriction |
| `tenureMonths: number` | **Redundant** — the product's "default" tenure, now expressible as a rate-card row | ⚠ Yes — a third opinion on tenure, currently read only by `formatSelectedTenure` for the Overview card | **Remove** once the rate card has a `isDefault` or the Overview card reads row 0. Lowest-risk deletion of the three |
| `minInvestment: number` | **Source of truth** | No | Keep |
| `maxInvestment: number` | **Source of truth** | No | Keep |
| `investmentIncrement?: number` | **Source of truth**, optional | No | Keep |
| `quickAmounts?: number[]` | **Source of truth** (candidates), filtered before use | No — every consumer filters | Keep |
| `tenureRange: { minDays, maxYears }` | **Display copy** — advertised family range | ⚠ Moderate — invites use as a selection bound | **Keep**, restrict reads (ALT-1). Explicitly *not* derivable from `tenureRates` |
| `tenureRates: TenureRate[]` | **Source of truth** — selectable tenures and their rates | No | Keep. Add `label?` (ALT-3) |
| `payoutOptions: string[]` | **Display copy** — bank's marketing words | ⚠ Moderate — same invitation | **Keep**, restrict reads. Not derivable without changing approved copy |
| `supportedPayouts: PayoutOptionId[]` | **Source of truth** | No | Keep |
| `prematureWithdrawal` | **Source of truth** | No | Keep |
| `autoRenewal: boolean` | **Source of truth** | No | Keep |

### 4.1 The rule this audit produces

> **`DepositTerms` carries two kinds of field: what the product *is* (source of
> truth, read by logic) and what the product *says about itself* (display copy,
> read only by row builders). No behavioural code path may read display copy.**

That sentence, plus one probe, is the whole fix. **One field should actually be
removed — `tenureMonths` — and the review did not notice it**, which is telling:
it recommended deleting three fields that should stay and missed the one that
should go.

## 5. Days vs Months — Five Designs

| | **A. `tenureMonths`** | **B. `tenureDays`** | **C. `{value, unit}`** | **D. ISO-8601 `"P24M"`** | **E. Value Object class** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Simplicity** | ★★★★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★ | ★★ | ★★★ |
| **Banking correctness** | ★★★ — cannot express 7 days | ★★★★★ — the unit banks settle on | ★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★★ |
| **Serialization** | ★★★★★ integer | ★★★★★ integer | ★★★ object | ★★★★ string, needs a parser JS lacks natively | ★★ needs custom (de)serialisation |
| **DB storage** | ★★★★★ indexable, sortable | ★★★★★ indexable, sortable | ★★ two columns, sorts wrongly across units | ★ **not sortable or comparable in SQL** | ★★★★★ (stores as its primitive) |
| **API evolution** | ★★★ unit change is breaking | ★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★★★ standard | ★★★★ |
| **Performance** | ★★★★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★ parse per comparison | ★★★★ |
| **Future scalability** | ★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★★★ |

**C, D and E share one fatal property: multiple representations of one
quantity.** `{12,'months'}`, `{1,'year'}` and `P1Y` are equal in the domain and
unequal as values, so equality, sorting, `Set` membership and deduplication all
break unless every consumer normalises first. If every consumer must normalise,
**the normalised form is the domain model** and the wrapper is presentation.
D adds a parser the language does not provide. E is orthogonal — a value object
must still choose A or B underneath.

### Recommendation — **A now, B on a trigger**

**Keep `tenureMonths`.** Migrate to `tenureDays` when the first product actually
sells a sub-month tenure. Take **ALT-3** (`label?` on `TenureRate`) immediately,
which delivers C/D/E's only genuine benefit — the bank's own wording — for zero
cost and zero ambiguity.

**This reverses the prior review's recommendation**, on three grounds it did not
account for: migration cost is constant rather than rising (D3); Maturity Date
does not depend on the unit (D4); and no product in scope sells a sub-month
deposit.

**Trigger to migrate:** the first `tenureRates` row that cannot be expressed in
whole months. Write it into the data contract so it is caught at ingestion.

## 7. Hidden Risks the Review Missed

| # | Risk | Severity | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H-1** | **Cached rate cards quoting stale rates.** TanStack Query will serve a cached product while the user configures a deposit against it. BR-PROD-003 requires notification on rate change; caching makes the stale window invisible | **High** | Real. Needs a staleness policy for pricing data distinct from catalogue data |
| **H-2** | **Time zones.** `Date.now()` drives draft expiry client-side; value date and maturity date will be date-typed. A booking at 23:30 IST is the previous day in UTC | **High** | Real and classic. Bank value dates are IST business days, not instants |
| **H-3** | **No correlation id on the client.** Doc 18 requires one per request for end-to-end tracing; the API client sends none | **Medium** | Cheap to add, painful to retrofit after an incident |
| **H-4** | **DICGC exposure is a product promise with no model.** Founder §6 names concentration and deposit-insurance awareness as a must-have differentiator. Insurance is **₹5 lakh per depositor per bank**, so it is a cross-product calculation nothing currently owns | **High** | The Founder Note's own feature list, unmodelled |
| **H-5** | **Localisation is hard-coded.** `formatCompactCurrency` emits lakh/crore; `LOCALE`/`CURRENCY` are constants. Multi-country breaks this entirely | **Low now** | Correct for India-only. Flag before international |
| **H-6** | **Business-day and holiday calendar.** Maturity on a Sunday or a bank holiday shifts. Unmodelled | **Medium** | Arrives with value dates |
| **H-7** | **No domain events on the client.** Doc 13 wants every transition auditable and event-emitting; the frontend records none | **Medium** | Matters at Payment/Processing, not here |
| **H-8** | **API versioning of the rate card.** No `version` or `issuedAt` on the payload shape | **Medium** | Fold into the R1 quote work |
| **H-9** | **Concurrency — assessed and clear.** Two tabs cannot corrupt each other: `sessionStorage` is per-tab. Had the earlier decision gone to `localStorage`, this would be a real last-write-wins defect | **None** | Worth recording as a risk the design already avoided |
| **H-10** | **Float precision — assessed and clear.** `Math.round(principal × growth)` in float64 is exact for rupee magnitudes up to ~9×10¹⁵; a ₹5 crore deposit is nowhere near it | **None** | Recorded so it is not raised again |

H-9 and H-10 are listed deliberately. A risk register that only grows is a
register nobody trusts.

## 8. Is This Premature?

**Yes — and the review contradicted its own author to get there.**

`t3.2-rebaseline.md` §4, risk **R-F3, severity High**, written before the review:

> *"Founder §6 names seven must-have PoC features — goal planner, suitability
> score, safety/concentration, FD ladder, post-tax, maturity alerts,
> explainability. **None is booking configuration.** §12 states the PoC will not
> be measured by screens completed."*

The review then required twelve modifications to booking configuration before a
tenure selector could be built. That is one to two engineering days spent
hardening the module the Founder Note asked to keep minimal, on a platform whose
headline differentiator — the goal planner — does not exist.

### What is actually needed, and when

| Horizon | Items |
|---|---|
| **Needed today (POC)** | M7 integer rupees · M9 rate-card uniqueness · ALT-1 read-restriction probe · ALT-2 headline consistency assertion · ALT-3 `label?` · `tenureRates` data |
| **Needed before MVP** | ALT-4 `compoundingFrequency` · Maturity Date + value date · applied-rate capture · H-3 correlation ids · H-1 pricing-cache policy |
| **Needed before production** | `RateQuote` / effective dating · day-count convention · H-2 time zones · H-6 business-day calendar · senior-citizen rates · BR-PROD-003 |
| **Needed after the first enterprise customer** | Amount-slab composition · payout-specific pricing · rate cards as a resource · H-7 domain events · H-5 localisation · multi-country |

**Today's list is roughly half a day.** The review's list was one to two days and
would have delayed the tenure selector — the last piece of the screen — behind
work for banks the platform has not signed.

## 9. Revised Verdict

**Approved with changes — five, not twelve.**

| # | Required before T3.2.10 | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **M7** — integer rupees in the draft schema and `validateAmount`. Ship standalone; do **not** bundle with a tenure migration | 15 min |
| **2** | **M9** — uniqueness in the rate-card data contract + assertion | 15 min |
| **3** | **ALT-1** — probe: no behavioural path reads `interestRate`, `tenureRange` or `payoutOptions` | 30 min |
| **4** | **ALT-2** — assertion: the headline rate appears in the rate card | 15 min |
| **5** | **`tenureRates` data** — one row minimum, six for the artwork | Product |

**Rejected:** M3 (`RateQuote` now) · M5 (delete `payoutOptions`) · M6 (delete
`tenureRange`) · M8 (bundled migration).

**Postponed with named triggers:** M1 `tenureDays` → first sub-month product ·
M2 day-count → with M1 · `RateQuote` → R1 or first second dimension ·
`useInvestmentConfiguration` → next change to the screen.

**Added, which the review missed:** remove `DepositTerms.tenureMonths`, the one
field that is genuinely redundant.

**Answer to "can T3.2.10 begin today":** yes, as soon as one row of rate data
exists. Items 1–4 total about ninety minutes and none of them blocks the control.
