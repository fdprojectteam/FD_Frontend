## C1 — Selectable tenure against a single interest rate ⚠ critical

**Sources in conflict**

| Priority | Source | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Spec 21b §C | Tenure is a primary control — dropdown, cards or radios |
| 2 | Spec 21b §9 | *"Investing for one more year unlocks a higher interest rate"* |
| 3 | Approved UI | Seven tenure chips + a slider, against one quoted rate of **7.25%** |
| 4 | Codebase, M2.6 decision D2 | Tenure was **fixed** on the Marketplace calculator, because *"a fixed deposit is a rate-for-tenure contract: this product has one rate and one tenure, so offering other tenures would quote a return the user cannot obtain"* |

**The conflict.** The domain model has one `interestRate` per product. Spec §9
asks the platform to compare rates *across* tenures, which requires a rate card
the platform does not have. Letting the user select a tenure while holding the
rate at 7.25% would quote an unobtainable return for six of the seven options —
on the screen where they commit money, and in exactly the way M2.6 refused.

**Why no default is safe.** Every available reading either quotes a wrong number
or contradicts an approved artefact. This is product data, not an engineering
choice.

**Options**

- **A (recommended)** — supply a tenure→rate card. Add
  `tenureRates: { tenureMonths, annualRate }[]` to `DepositTerms`. Booking owns
  its own types, so **Marketplace is untouched**. Makes §9's guidance truthful
  when it is approved.
- **B** — one rate for all tenures. Cheapest; quotes returns that cannot be
  obtained.
- **C** — tenure is display-only, as on the Marketplace calculator. Honest, but
  contradicts the approved artwork, where tenure is the primary control.

**Needed:** the rate card, or a decision to accept B or C.

## C2 — The approved screen's own figures do not reconcile ⚠ critical

**Sources in conflict:** the approved UI (Priority 3) against the platform's
maturity formula (Priority 4, derived and verified in M2.1).

The artwork states ₹5,00,000 · 7.25% · 24 months · **Total Interest ₹66,478** ·
**Maturity ₹5,66,478**. Computed:

| Basis | Maturity | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| **Quarterly compounding** (platform formula, M2.1) | ₹5,77,270 | ₹77,270 |
| Monthly compounding | ₹5,77,768 | ₹77,768 |
| Annual compounding | ₹5,75,128 | ₹75,128 |
| Simple interest | ₹5,72,500 | ₹72,500 |
| **Artwork** | **₹5,66,478** | **₹66,478** |

No basis reproduces the artwork. Its figure implies **6.29%** compounded
quarterly, or **6.648%** simple — neither is the 7.25% printed beside it.

The platform formula is not in doubt: M2.1 derived it and matched all six
published Marketplace figures exactly (IDFC, HDFC, Kotak, SBI, ICICI, Axis).

**The risk if guessed.** Booking and Marketplace would quote different maturity
values for the same deposit. That is a correctness defect on a financial
product, not a styling inconsistency.

**Status:** escalated to design 2026-08-06 by your instruction. **T3.2.5 and
T3.2.8 are halted.** The rest of the screen is unaffected — the configured right
rail shows rate, tenure and payout, not returns.

**Needed:** confirmation that the mockup figures are illustrative and the
platform formula stands; or, if Booking is to use a different basis, that basis
stated explicitly. **It will not be reverse-engineered.**

## C3 — Quick Select chips: additive or absolute? ⚠ blocking

**Sources in conflict**

- **Approved UI** — three chips labelled `+ ₹1L`, `+ ₹5L`, `+ ₹10L`. The `+`
  reads as *add*.
- **Spec 21b §B** — *"Quick Amount Chips: ₹50K, ₹1L, ₹2L, ₹5L, ₹10L"* — five
  chips, absolute values, no `+`.
- **The artwork again** — `+ ₹5L` is drawn in the **selected** state while the
  amount field reads ₹5,00,000. That is what an *absolute* chip looks like after
  being pressed, and is not a state an additive chip should hold at all.

Three artefacts, three readings, and they disagree about both the count and the
behaviour.

**Options**

- **A** — additive: `+ ₹5L` adds ₹5,00,000 to the current amount, clamped to the
  product maximum. Matches the labels; the selected styling becomes transient.
- **B** — absolute: chips set the amount, selection reflects a match. Matches the
  spec and explains the artwork's selected state; contradicts the `+`.
- **C** — additive, with the pressed styling shown only while active.

**No recommendation offered.** On a money field the difference between "set to
₹5L" and "add ₹5L" is the difference between a ₹5,00,000 and a ₹10,00,000
deposit. This needs design's answer.

## C4 — Non-cumulative payout is undefined ⚠ blocking

**Source:** spec 21b §D and §E define the payout options and the returns
display, but never state what "Maturity Amount" means when interest is *paid
out* rather than compounded. The approved artwork shows only the `At Maturity`
case.

For Monthly or Quarterly payout the principal is returned at maturity and
interest is paid periodically — so `Maturity Amount = Principal`, and the
approved four-cell strip would show ₹5,00,000 where it currently shows
₹5,66,478.

**Options**

- **A (recommended)** — non-cumulative payouts use simple interest on the
  principal at the selected frequency; `Maturity Amount = Principal`;
  `Total Interest` is the total paid over the term; the strip gains a
  per-instalment line (e.g. *"₹3,020 / month"*). A copy change, not a layout
  change.
- **B** — show compounded figures regardless of payout. Simpler, and tells a
  monthly-payout investor they will receive ₹5,66,478 at maturity when they will
  receive ₹5,00,000.

**Needed:** confirmation of A, and of whether the per-instalment line is wanted.

## C5 — `Custom` tenure control is unspecified

**Source:** the approved artwork shows a `Custom` chip carrying a **calendar**
icon. The spec does not describe what it opens.

A calendar picks a *date*; tenure is a *duration*. Asking the user to compute a
duration from two dates is the opposite of the plain-English, senior-usable
direction in Founder Note §4.

**Proposed:** `Custom` reveals an **inline month field**, constrained to the
product's tenure range — no dialog, no date picker. Non-blocking for T3.2.4;
blocking for T3.2.7.

## C6 — Post-tax display has no data source *(resolved 2026-08-06)*

Founder Note §6 requires an estimated post-tax return; the approved artwork has
no cell for one, and Founder §5 step 3 places *tax bracket/range* among the
**goal-led planner's** inputs — a module that does not exist.

**Resolved:** wait for the planner. T3.2 builds the seam (`taxProfile?` on the
returns derivation) and renders no post-tax figure until a bracket is supplied.
No invented default; no fourth control on a screen the Note wants light.

## C7 — Four steps versus eight *(resolved 2026-08-06, implemented)*

Founder Note §7 requires four booking steps; the approved UI, doc 20 and the
shipped `BOOKING_STEPS` specify eight.

**Resolved:** four phases in the stepper, eight routes underneath. Implemented
in T3.2.1. Recorded that this satisfies the Note's labelling more fully than its
intent — the user still visits eight screens — so the lightness requirement is
carried by the screens themselves.

## C8 — Marketplace owns a formula Booking needs *(constraint, not a conflict)*

`calculateMaturity` lives in `src/modules/marketplace/utils/maturity.ts`. Booking
cannot import it (module boundary, ESLint-enforced), and Priority 4 forbids
modifying Marketplace without explicit instruction. CLAUDE.md says promote
rather than duplicate; under the no-touch rule both cannot hold.

**Planned (D10):** add `@/utils/maturity.ts` as a *new platform file* — adding a
platform file is not modifying Marketplace — and leave Marketplace's copy alone,
with a unit assertion running both over the six published figures so they cannot
drift silently.

**Recorded as knowingly imperfect.** Two copies of a money formula is worse
architecture than one; the drift risk is contained by a test rather than
removed. One instruction converges them. Blocked behind C2 in any case.
